# Carom

A paddle, a ball, and a shot that refuses to fly straight. Carom is a fast,
top-down duel where every moving strike adds spin, bending the next flight
through a dark court lit in mint, violet, and amber.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your rhythm. Meet the ball squarely
for a clean return, catch it near a tip for a sharper angle, or sweep your paddle
through contact to send a curving shot across the field. The longer a rally
lasts, the faster it gets.

Two amber bars sway and rotate between you and your opponent. Their tilted faces
can turn a safe return into a bank shot—or send your own clever plan straight
back at you. Read the changing gaps, keep moving, and make the obstacles work
for your side of the net.

Take on the computer in Solo or challenge a friend beside you in Versus. Race to
11 with a two-point lead, then stay composed when the score reaches deuce: one
well-timed curve can turn match point into another rally.
